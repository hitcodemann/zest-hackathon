## Slide 2: The Problem Statement
**"Banking is Boring. Finance is Scary."**

*   **Disengagement**: Traditional banking apps are utilitarian, text-heavy, and lack emotional connection. They feel like "chores."
*   **Low Financial Literacy**: Gen Z/Alpha learns from TikTok/Reels, not spreadsheets. Current tools don't speak their language.
*   **Instant Gratification Culture**: The "Buy Now, Pay Later" mindset is leading to debt. There's no immediate dopamine hit for *saving*.
*   **The Gap**: There is no financial tool that matches the *vibe*, *speed*, and *personalization* of the apps Gen Z actually uses (TikTok, Snapchat, Instagram).

---

## Slide 3: The Solution - Meet "Zest"
**A Financial Companion that Passes the Vibe Check.**

Zest is not just a bank; it's a **gamified, AI-powered lifestyle app** that makes money management addictive and fun.

*   **Gen Z Native UI**: Dark mode, neon aesthetics, swipeable interfaces, and micro-animations.
*   **Hyper-Personalized**: Uses AI to understand user habits and goals.
*   **Gamified**: Turns saving into a game with XP, streaks, and quests.

---

## Slide 4: Solution Architecture
**Built for Speed, Scale, and Intelligence.**

*   **Frontend**: **Next.js (React)** for a high-performance, mobile-first web application.
*   **AI Layer**: **Google Gemini 2.0 Flash** API.
    *   Powers the "Money Twin" chat for real-time, context-aware advice.
    *   Analyzes spending patterns to generate "Agentic" proactive suggestions.
*   **State Management**: React Context + LocalStorage (Prototype) for instant, offline-capable interactions.
*   **Agentic System**: Background workers (`AgentContext`) that monitor user state (Goals vs. Spending) to trigger timely interventions.

---

## Slide 5: Key Features
1.  **Money Twin (AI Agent)**: A chatbot that speaks Gen Z slang ("No cap", "Bet"), roasts bad spending, and hypes up savings.
2.  **Dynamic Quests**: Daily challenges generated by AI based on your actual spending habits (e.g., "Skip the coffee today").
3.  **Goal Achiever**: Visual goal tracking with "Smart Move" recommendations to invest savings upon completion.
4.  **Marketplace 2.0**: Simplified SIPs/FDs and XP redemption for real-world rewards (Vouchers).

---

## Slide 6: Business Value & Relevance
**Why Zest Wins.**

*   **Relevance**:
    *   Speaks the language of the target demographic (Slang, Memes, UI trends).
    *   Meets them where they are: On their phones, looking for quick, bite-sized interactions.
*   **Practicality**:
    *   **Agentic AI** removes the cognitive load of planning. The app *tells you* what to do (e.g., "You're ₹500 short for your trip, save ₹50 today").
    *   **One-Tap Investing**: Lowers the barrier to entry for financial products (SIPs/FDs).
*   **Value to Users**:
    *   **Financial Wellness**: Builds good habits through positive reinforcement (Dopamine hits).
    *   **Education**: Teaches finance through doing, not reading.
    *   **Rewards**: Tangible benefits (Vouchers) for good behavior.
